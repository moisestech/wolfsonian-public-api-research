## Progress after two four-room expansions

PR #56 joined all 23 existing speakers into a single connected public-preview society. This batch adds the Sparton 558-C radio, the 1927 Machine Age Exposition catalogue, and Mappemonde as separately identified **public-packet** speakers. Four new artist-proposed exchanges link them to existing rooms; the working count becomes **31 conversations / 26 speakers / one connected group**.

These three packets were manually prepared from museum-published sources; they are **not** confirmed Lakehouse matches, institutionally reviewed scripts or rights-cleared collection photos. Exact accessions and museum source links remain inspectable in each room. Five further conversations and at least two further source-ready objects remain for the 36 / 28 exhibition target.

A visitor test succeeds when four of five people can follow a speaker into two further conversations without coaching and identify one detail from the record alongside one imagined line. These targets are working hypotheses. Capture the chosen rooms and where each person hesitates before expanding beyond 36.

For each batch, keep a source locator per object, identify any line requiring more evidence, and label the basis of the pairing. Use existing public object titles for title-based readings. Staff tags, proposed comparisons and documented contact have different evidentiary requirements. Physical storage belongs to the Annex view.
