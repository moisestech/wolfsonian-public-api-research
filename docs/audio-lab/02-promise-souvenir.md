## Fixed script — ElevenLabs multi-speaker / editable audio master
Each turn below is an **original fictional line written for this experiment**. Do not claim the actual objects spoke or the museum approved these lines. Delivery directions are optional performance cues, not part of the archival record.

01. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(opening)_: I address you directly: Your World of Tomorrow.

02. **Booklet, Futurama** _(tease)_: I arrive with a shorter name: Futurama. Almost like a place you can take home.

03. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(pushback)_: But a program is also something you can keep.

04. **Booklet, Futurama** _(quick)_: Sure. Keeping a printed future isn't the same as receiving it.

05. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(suspend)_: So whose future is it, once the date on the paper has passed?

06. **Booklet, Futurama** _(self_correct)_: I don't know. I can't even say who kept me.

07. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(insight)_: Then perhaps we preserve not the future, but somebody's way of selling it.

08. **Booklet, Futurama** _(soft_end)_: And the question of what happened after the promise.
