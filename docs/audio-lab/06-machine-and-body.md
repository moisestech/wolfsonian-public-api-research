02. **Grind Safely. One Splinter Can Cost You an Eye** _(interrupt)_: [interrupting] Not if you are the eye beside the grinding wheel.

03. **Making the Engine** _(challenge)_: You sound like a warning.

04. **Grind Safely. One Splinter Can Cost You an Eye** _(precise)_: I am a warning. My title says what one splinter could cost.

08. **Making the Engine** _(end)_: And whose work was missing when we praised the engine.
