## Fixed script — ElevenLabs multi-speaker / editable audio master
Each turn below is an **original fictional line written for this experiment**. Do not claim the actual objects spoke or the museum approved these lines. Delivery directions are optional performance cues, not part of the archival record.

01. **Vase with lid, Mappemonde [Globe]** _(opening_slow)_: I fit a world into the shape of a vessel.

02. **Model, Trylon and Perisphere** _(soft)_: I make a world's fair appear small enough to study as a model.

03. **Vase with lid, Mappemonde [Globe]** _(pause)_: Small enough to arrange. Small enough to own?

04. **Model, Trylon and Perisphere** _(caution)_: [hesitates] A model invites that feeling. But it doesn't give anyone the whole world.

05. **Vase with lid, Mappemonde [Globe]** _(insight)_: Neither does my globe. There is always a side you cannot see from here.

06. **Model, Trylon and Perisphere** _(measured)_: And people beyond the edges of an exhibition.

07. **Vase with lid, Mappemonde [Globe]** _(reveal)_: So perhaps scale is not only what you make visible.

08. **Model, Trylon and Perisphere** _(closing)_: It's what you're willing to admit remains outside.
