## Fixed script — ElevenLabs multi-speaker / editable audio master
Each turn below is an **original fictional line written for this experiment**. Do not claim the actual objects spoke or the museum approved these lines. Delivery directions are optional performance cues, not part of the archival record.

01. **Poster, In 1939: The New York World’s Fair** _(open)_: In my picture, there is already a crowd approaching tomorrow.

02. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(simple)_: But your crowd is printed.

03. **Poster, In 1939: The New York World’s Fair** _(pacing)_: Yes. That's the trick. An invitation can show its audience before they arrive.

04. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(emphasis)_: My title says 'Your World of Tomorrow'. That little word, 'your', is doing a lot of work.

05. **Poster, In 1939: The New York World’s Fair** _(curious)_: You mean it sounds as though everyone is included?

06. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(correction)_: It addresses someone. It doesn't identify who entered.

07. **Poster, In 1939: The New York World’s Fair** _(realize)_: Then a painted line is not a list of visitors.

08. **Program, Your World of Tomorrow** _(end)_: What would an actual attendance record tell us that neither of us can?
