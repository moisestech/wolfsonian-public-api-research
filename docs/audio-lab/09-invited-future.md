## Fixed script — ElevenLabs multi-speaker / editable audio master
Each turn below is an **original fictional line written for this experiment**. Do not claim the actual objects spoke or the museum approved these lines. Delivery directions are optional performance cues, not part of the archival record.

01. **Harlem Invites You to Visit the New York World’s Fair (leaflet)** _(opening)_: My first word is Harlem. An invitation tells you where the route begins.

02. **Futurama (1939 book spread)** _(scene)_: My city can be seen from above, with a narration guiding your view.

03. **Harlem Invites You to Visit the New York World’s Fair (leaflet)** _(ask)_: Who chooses the path?

04. **Futurama (1939 book spread)** _(measured)_: In the attraction described by the checklist, the presentation sets it out.

05. **Harlem Invites You to Visit the New York World’s Fair (leaflet)** _(interrupt)_: [interrupting] And who gets to decide whether that promised future includes them?

06. **Futurama (1939 book spread)** _(self_correct)_: I can't answer for anyone who visited. Our records don't establish that.

07. **Harlem Invites You to Visit the New York World’s Fair (leaflet)** _(clarify)_: Then let's not confuse a designed view with a lived experience.

08. **Futurama (1939 book spread)** _(end)_: Let's ask for the voices of people who actually traveled to the fair.
