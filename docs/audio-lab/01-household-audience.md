## Fixed script — ElevenLabs multi-speaker / editable audio master
Each turn below is an **original fictional line written for this experiment**. Do not claim the actual objects spoke or the museum approved these lines. Delivery directions are optional performance cues, not part of the archival record.

01. **Television, RCA Victor TRK 12** _(opening)_: If you put me in a room, people could watch a picture.

02. **Radio, Sparton, model 558-C** _(interrupt)_: [interrupting] And if you put me in a room, they might imagine one.

03. **Television, RCA Victor TRK 12** _(challenge)_: So you think the imagination belongs to the radio?

04. **Radio, Sparton, model 558-C** _(measured)_: Not exactly. A voice leaves a lot of space around itself.

05. **Television, RCA Victor TRK 12** _(correction)_: A screen leaves questions too. We don't know what any particular person watched.

06. **Radio, Sparton, model 558-C** _(warm)_: Or what they heard through me. Our beautiful cabinets are not audience diaries.

07. **Television, RCA Victor TRK 12** _(insight)_: Then the room we're missing isn't furniture. It's a person.

08. **Radio, Sparton, model 558-C** _(question)_: Whose account would let that person speak for themselves?
